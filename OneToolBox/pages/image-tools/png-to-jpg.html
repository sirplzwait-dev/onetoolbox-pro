❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert PNG to JPG?

Upload a PNG, choose quality and background, then click Convert to JPG.

Can I convert a transparent PNG?

Yes. White Background is enabled by default. Uncheck it to render transparent areas black.

Can I control JPG quality?

Yes. Use the quality slider or quick quality buttons.

Will dimensions change?

No. The JPG keeps the original width and height.

Does JPG support transparency?

No. JPG has no transparency channel.

Are images uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion is performed in the browser with Canvas.

Is this converter free?

Yes.
