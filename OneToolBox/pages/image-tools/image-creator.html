Frequently Asked Questions

Can I move objects?

Yes. Click an object and drag it anywhere on the canvas.

Can I resize an object?

Yes. Select an object and use the size control where supported. Uploaded images and shapes can also be adjusted with the resize handle.

Can I add my own image?

Yes. PNG, JPG and WebP images can be added to the canvas.

Can I change the canvas background?

Yes. Use the Background color picker.

Does the tool upload my files?

No. Image editing is performed locally in your browser.
