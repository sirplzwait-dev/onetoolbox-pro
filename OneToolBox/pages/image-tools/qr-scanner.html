Frequently Asked Questions

Is QR Scanner free?

Yes. It is designed to work in the browser without an API key.

Does the camera work on mobile?

Yes, if the browser supports camera access and the page is served over HTTPS or localhost.

Can I scan a QR code from a photo?

Yes. Use the Image tab and upload a JPG, PNG or WebP image.

Is my QR data uploaded?

No. The QR decoding code runs locally in your browser.

Why does camera scanning not start?

Check camera permission and make sure the website is running on HTTPS or localhost. Some browsers may not support the native QR detector used for camera scanning.
