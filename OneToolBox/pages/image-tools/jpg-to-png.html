❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert JPG to PNG?

Upload your JPG or JPEG image, choose the desired compression setting and click Convert to PNG. Your PNG image will then be available to download.

Can I convert JPEG to PNG?

Yes. Both JPG and JPEG images are supported by this converter.

Will the image dimensions change?

No. The converted PNG keeps the original image width and height.

Does JPG to PNG improve image quality?

Converting JPG to PNG does not restore quality that was already lost in the original JPG. PNG is a different image format and is generally lossless for the converted image data.

Can I compress the PNG?

Yes. You can select the available compression setting before conversion.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion is performed directly in your browser.

Is this JPG to PNG converter free?

Yes. You can convert JPG and JPEG images to PNG without installing additional software.
