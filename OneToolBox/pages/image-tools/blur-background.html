Frequently Asked Questions

Can I blur only the background?

Yes. The tool detects the main person and keeps the subject sharp while applying blur to the surrounding area.

Does the image leave my device?

Image processing is performed locally in your browser after the required AI model has loaded.

Can I change the blur intensity?

Yes. Use the Blur Intensity slider from 0 to 50 pixels.

Can I download JPG?

Yes. You can choose PNG or JPG and adjust JPG quality before downloading.

Why can the first processing take time?

The browser may need to download the AI segmentation model the first time. Subsequent processing can be faster.
