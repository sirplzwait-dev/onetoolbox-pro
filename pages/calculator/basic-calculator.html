Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the calculator.

Is the Basic Calculator free?

Yes. It is designed to work directly in your browser.

Does it need a file upload?

No. Enter numbers or an expression directly into the calculator.

Does it support scientific calculations?

Yes. It includes sin, cos, tan, log, ln, square root, powers, π and e.

Can I use my keyboard?

Yes. Enter calculates, Backspace deletes and Esc clears.
