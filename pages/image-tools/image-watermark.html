Frequently Asked Questions

Is this watermark tool free?

Yes. It works directly in your browser and does not require an API.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Select Logo and upload your PNG, JPG or WebP logo.

Can I move the watermark?

Yes. Drag the watermark directly on the preview image or use the quick position buttons.

Can I change opacity and rotation?

Yes. Use the Opacity and Rotation controls.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The image and watermark are processed locally in your browser.
