Frequently Asked Questions

Does the background really get removed first?

Yes. The tool first creates a transparent foreground cutout. The new background is added only after that step.

Can I use a custom color?

Yes. Pick any color using the color picker or enter its HEX value.

Can I use another image?

Yes. Choose Image and upload the picture you want behind the subject.

Is the background removal AI based?

Yes. The browser uses IMG.LY's background-removal model for foreground segmentation.

Will hair and fine edges always be perfect?

AI segmentation is strong but can vary with difficult hair, glass, shadows and very complex backgrounds.

Are my images uploaded?

The background-removal model runs in the browser; the image itself is processed on your device.
