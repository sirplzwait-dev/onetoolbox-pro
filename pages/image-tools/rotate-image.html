❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I rotate an image online?

Upload your image, choose the desired rotation angle and click Rotate Image. The result will appear in the result section.

Can I rotate an image by 90 degrees?

Yes. Use Rotate Left or Rotate Right to rotate the image by 90 degrees.

Can I use a custom rotation angle?

Yes. The rotation slider supports angles from -180° to +180°.

Can I flip my image?

Yes. You can flip the image horizontally or vertically.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG and WEBP output formats are supported.

Can I control image quality?

Yes. Use the Quality slider before applying the rotation.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing happens directly in your browser.

Is this tool free?

Yes. You can rotate images online without installing software.
