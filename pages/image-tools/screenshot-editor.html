Frequently Asked Questions

Does the editor upload my screenshot?

No. The editor processes the image in your browser.

Can I add text and arrows?

Yes. Select Text, Arrow, Rectangle, Circle or other annotation tools.

Can I crop and rotate screenshots?

Yes. Use Crop, Rotate Left, Rotate Right, Flip H and Flip V.

Can I download JPG?

Yes. You can download the finished image as PNG or JPG.

Is it mobile friendly?

Yes. The layout adapts to smaller screens.
