Frequently Asked Questions

What does PNG to JPG do?

It converts a PNG image into JPG format.

Why is a background needed?

JPG does not support transparency, so transparent PNG areas need a background.

Can I change JPG quality?

Yes. Use the quality slider before converting.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes, the page is responsive for mobile screens.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Conversion happens locally in your browser.
