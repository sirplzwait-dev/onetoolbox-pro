Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image Collage Maker free?

Yes. It runs directly in your browser and does not require an API.

How many images can I add?

You can add up to 9 images at a time.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Images are processed locally in your browser.

Can I change the collage background?

Yes. Choose any background color using the color picker.

Can I download the collage?

Yes. Download it as PNG or JPG.
