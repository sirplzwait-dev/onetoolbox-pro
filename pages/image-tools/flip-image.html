❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I flip an image online?

Upload your image, select Horizontal or Vertical Flip and click Flip Image. Your result will appear in the result section.

What is horizontal image flipping?

Horizontal flipping creates a mirror effect by reversing the image from left to right.

What is vertical image flipping?

Vertical flipping reverses the image from top to bottom.

Can I flip an image both horizontally and vertically?

Yes. Use the Flip Both option to apply both directions together.

Which formats are supported?

You can export your flipped image as JPG, PNG or WEBP.

Can I control the image quality?

Yes. Use the Quality slider before applying the flip.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The image is processed directly in your browser using JavaScript.

Is the Flip Image tool free?

Yes. You can flip images online without installing additional software.
