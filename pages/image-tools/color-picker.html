Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pick a color?

Upload an image and click or tap anywhere on the image. The tool reads the exact pixel under your pointer.

Can I copy the HEX value?

Yes. Click the HEX, RGB or HSL result card to copy that value.

Can I pick a color directly from my screen?

On supported browsers, the Pick From Screen button uses the native EyeDropper API. If it is unavailable, use the uploaded-image picker.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Tap the image to select a color.

Are my images uploaded?

No server upload is required for image color sampling. The image is decoded and sampled in your browser.
