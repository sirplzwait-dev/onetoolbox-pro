❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What does this Photo Enhancer do?

It lets you adjust brightness, contrast, saturation, sharpness and overall enhancement level of your image.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images are supported.

Can I enhance a dark photo?

Yes. Increase the brightness and contrast to improve the appearance of a dark image.

Can I make a photo sharper?

Yes. Increase the Sharpness control and preview the result before downloading.

What are the enhancement presets?

Natural, Enhanced, Vivid and Sharp provide quick starting settings for different types of photos.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. The basic enhancement is performed directly in your browser.
