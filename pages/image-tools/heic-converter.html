❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a HEIC image?

HEIC is an image format commonly used by modern Apple devices such as iPhones and iPads.

Can I convert HEIC to JPG?

Yes. Upload your HEIC image and click Convert to JPG.

Can I control JPG quality?

Yes. You can use the quality slider or quick quality buttons.

Will image dimensions change?

No. The converter keeps the original image dimensions.

Is HEIF also supported?

Yes. HEIF files are supported by the converter.
