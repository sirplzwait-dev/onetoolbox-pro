Frequently Asked Questions

How many images can I convert?

You can select multiple images and combine them into one PDF. The practical limit depends on your device memory.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP images are supported.

Can I create an A4 PDF?

Yes. A4 is selected by default. A3, Letter, Legal and Auto are also available.

Can I change PDF orientation?

Yes. You can switch between Portrait and Landscape.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

This tool creates the PDF in your browser using jsPDF. Your selected images are not sent to a OneToolBox server by this page.
