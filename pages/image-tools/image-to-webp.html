❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is WebP?

WebP is a modern image format designed to provide smaller image files while maintaining good visual quality.

Which images can I convert?

You can upload JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images.

Does WebP support transparency?

Yes. WebP supports transparency, so transparent PNG areas can be preserved.

Can I change WebP quality?

Yes. Use the quality slider or the quick quality buttons.

Will image dimensions change?

No. The converter keeps the original image dimensions.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion is performed directly in your browser using the Canvas API.

Is the WebP converter free?

Yes. You can convert images to WebP directly from your browser.
