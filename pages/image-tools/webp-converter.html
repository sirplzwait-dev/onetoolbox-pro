Frequently Asked Questions

What can I convert WEBP to?

You can convert WEBP images to JPG or PNG using the format buttons.

Which format should I choose?

JPG is useful for smaller photographic images. PNG is useful when you want lossless output.

Can I change JPG quality?

Yes. The quality slider is used when JPG is selected.

What happens to transparent areas?

JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas use the selected background color. PNG can preserve transparency.

Can I use this on mobile?

Yes. The converter is responsive and designed for mobile screens too.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Conversion is performed locally in your browser.
