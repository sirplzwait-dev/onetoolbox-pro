How Does the Image Cropper Work?

Upload an image, choose a ratio, adjust the crop and transform controls, then export the result.

1

Upload an Image

Choose an image, drag it into the upload area or paste it from your clipboard.

2

3

Crop & Download

Mobile Friendly

Privacy & File Handling

The current crop workflow uses Cropper.js and browser canvas APIs to process the selected image in the browser.

No server upload is required for the crop operation itself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I crop an image to 1:1?

Yes. Select the 1:1 ratio for a square crop.

Can I rotate and flip the image?

Yes. You can rotate left or right, use the rotation slider and flip horizontally or vertically.

Can I zoom and move the image?

Yes. Use the zoom buttons or slider and move the image inside the crop area.

Can I choose the output format?

Yes. JPG, PNG and WEBP are available, with a quality control for the exported image.

Can I drag and drop or paste an image?

Yes. The tool supports file selection, drag-and-drop and clipboard image paste.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

The crop operation itself is performed in the browser with Cropper.js and canvas APIs.
