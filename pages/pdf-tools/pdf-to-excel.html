FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about PDF To Excel.

Is PDF To Excel free?

Yes. This browser-based version is designed for free use.

Can it convert PDF tables?

It can extract selectable PDF text and organize it into spreadsheet rows. Complex tables may require manual cleanup.

Can I convert scanned PDFs?

Scanned PDFs contain images rather than selectable text. OCR is required for reliable text extraction from scanned documents.

Will the original PDF layout be preserved?

No. The tool focuses on extracting content into an editable spreadsheet rather than reproducing the exact PDF design.
