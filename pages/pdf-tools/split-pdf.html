FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I split every page separately?

Yes. Select “Every Page” and each page will become a separate PDF.

Can I split a PDF into groups of pages?

Yes. Select “Every N Pages” and choose how many pages should be placed in each PDF.

Can I choose custom page ranges?

Yes. Enter ranges such as 1-3, 4-6, 7-10. Each range becomes a separate PDF.

Does the original PDF change?

No. The original PDF is not modified.
