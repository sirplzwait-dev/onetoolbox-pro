FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add a logo as a watermark?

Yes. Switch to Photo mode and select a PNG, JPG or WebP image.

Can I use a transparent PNG?

Yes. Transparent PNG is recommended for logos and signatures.

Can I see the watermark before creating the PDF?

Yes. The first page is shown in Live Preview and updates with your settings.

Can I watermark selected pages?

Yes. Use values such as 1, 3, 5-7.
