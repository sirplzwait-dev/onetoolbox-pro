FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert multiple JPG images into one PDF?

Yes. Select multiple images and they will be placed into a single PDF in the selected order.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG and WebP images are supported by the tool.

Can I choose A4 size?

Yes. A4, Letter and Fit Image page options are available.

Can I change the image order?

Images are added to the PDF in the order selected by the browser. You can select them in the desired order where your browser supports ordered multi-selection.
