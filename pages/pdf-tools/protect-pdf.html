FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add a password to a PDF?

Yes. The PDF is encrypted with QPDF WebAssembly directly in your browser.

Can I control printing and copying?

Yes. You can choose printing, copying and modification permissions.

Is the original PDF changed?

No. The original file remains unchanged and a new protected PDF is created.

Does my PDF get uploaded?

No. The PDF is processed in browser memory. The QPDF engine itself is loaded from the CDN.
