FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I remove multiple pages at once?

Yes. Enter pages such as 2, 4, 7-9 to remove multiple pages and ranges.

What happens to the original PDF?

The original file remains unchanged. A new PDF is created without the selected pages.

Can I remove the first or last page quickly?

Yes. Use the quick buttons for the first or last page.

Can I remove every page?

No. At least one page must remain in the output PDF.
