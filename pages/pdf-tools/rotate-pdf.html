FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I rotate all pages at once?

Yes. Select All Pages and choose 90°, 180° or 270°.

Can I rotate only selected pages?

Yes. Select Custom Pages and enter page numbers or ranges such as 1, 3, 5-7.

Can I rotate odd or even pages?

Yes. Odd Pages and Even Pages are available.

Will my original PDF be changed?

No. A new rotated PDF is created and the original remains unchanged.
