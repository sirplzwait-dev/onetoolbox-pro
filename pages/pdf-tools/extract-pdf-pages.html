FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract multiple pages?

Yes. Enter multiple page numbers or ranges such as 1, 3, 5-7.

Can I extract a page range?

Yes. For example, 5-10 extracts pages 5 through 10.

Will the original PDF change?

No. The original PDF remains unchanged and a new PDF is created.

Can I extract all pages?

Yes. Use the All Pages quick option.
